 🛍️ Consumer Shopping Behavior Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **consumer shopping behavior** for a retail company to uncover trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies.
By leveraging **Python, SQL, and Power BI**, the project transforms raw data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

 🎯 Business Objective

To answer the core business question:

> **“How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?”**

The analysis explores customer demographics, purchasing patterns, product categories, discounts, reviews, seasons, and payment preferences.

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies Used

* **Python** – Data cleaning, preprocessing, and modeling
* **SQL** – Data organization, business queries, and insight extraction
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and visual storytelling
